Privacy Summary: Light Uprising does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. All communication happens directly between your device and your Bluetooth lights. No cloud services, no analytics, no tracking.

Introduction

Light Uprising ("we," "our," or "the app") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information in connection with your use of the Light Uprising iOS application.

Information We Do Not Collect

Light Uprising is designed with privacy as a core principle. We want to be clear about what we do not do:

  • No Personal Information: We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, or any other personally identifiable information.
  • No Usage Analytics: We do not track how you use the app, which features you access, or how often you use the app.
  • No Location Data: While Bluetooth functionality may have location permissions on iOS, we do not access, collect, or store your location data.
  • No Device Information: We do not collect information about your iOS device, model, or operating system version.
  • No Third-Party Analytics: We do not use any third-party analytics services, tracking SDKs, or advertising networks.
  • No Cloud Storage: We do not store any data on remote servers or in the cloud.

How the App Works

Light Uprising uses Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE) to communicate directly with your smart lights:

  1. The app scans for nearby BLE devices advertising as compatible lights
  2. When you connect to a light, communication happens directly between your iOS device and the light
  3. Color changes, brightness adjustments, and mode selections are sent directly to your lights via Bluetooth
  4. No data leaves your device except for the Bluetooth commands sent to your lights

All processing happens locally on your device. There are no servers involved, no internet connection required (except to download the app), and no data transmission beyond direct Bluetooth communication with your lights.

Bluetooth Permissions

Light Uprising requires Bluetooth access to function. iOS requires apps to request permission to use Bluetooth, and on some iOS versions, this includes location permissions as a system requirement (even though we don't access location data).

The app uses Bluetooth solely to:

  • Scan for compatible BLE light devices
  • Connect to lights you select
  • Send control commands (color, brightness, mode, power) to connected lights

Data Storage

Light Uprising may store minimal data locally on your device, such as:

  • App preferences and settings
  • Recently connected device names (stored only on your device)

This data never leaves your device and can be deleted by removing the app.

Third-Party Services

Light Uprising does not integrate with any third-party services, SDKs, or APIs. The app is completely self-contained and operates independently.

Children's Privacy

Light Uprising does not collect any information from anyone, including children under the age of 13. The app is safe for users of all ages.
