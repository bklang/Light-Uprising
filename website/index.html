How It Works

Light Uprising makes controlling your Bluetooth lights simple and intuitive:

  • Open the app and it automatically scans for compatible BLE lights
  • See all discovered lights with their current colors and signal strength
  • Tap any light to connect and access full controls
  • Change colors, adjust brightness, switch modes, and power on/off
  • All changes happen instantly with no lag

Requirements

Light Uprising is designed for iOS devices running iOS 17.0 or later. Your device must support Bluetooth Low Energy (all modern iPhones and iPads do).

The app works with BLE lights that advertise with the standard lighting service UUID. Check with your light manufacturer for compatibility.
